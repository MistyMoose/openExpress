openExpress for Mac

openExpress is a free replacement for Garmin Express on Apple silicon Macs: it backs up and syncs your Garmin watch or bike computer and installs its software, maps and apps.

openExpress is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Garmin. Garmin and fēnix are trademarks of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries.

  • Quit Garmin Express first, including "Garmin Express Service" in the menu bar: while it runs it holds the device, and openExpress cannot reach it.
  • openExpress for Mac is installed in the Applications folder. It needs macOS 26 or later.
